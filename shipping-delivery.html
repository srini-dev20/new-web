Shipping / Digital Delivery Policy

Prashq provides digital services only. No physical products are sold or shipped.

Prashq is a service brand operated by Srinivasan Ranganathan, based in India.

Services may include Framer landing-page design and development, responsive desktop and mobile implementation, light copy cleanup, CTA integration, basic SEO/social metadata setup, preview links, handoff videos, and related digital deliverables.

Project timelines are agreed before work begins. A typical landing-page sprint is completed within 5 working days after upfront payment, required project assets, and scope confirmation are received.

Delivery timelines may change if required assets, access, feedback, approvals, or project information are delayed.

During the project, work may be shared through preview links, screenshots, Loom-style videos, documents, or other digital communication methods.

Final delivery may include Framer project transfer, domain publishing support, editable access, handoff video, and related digital materials, depending on the agreed scope.

Final handoff is completed after the remaining balance is paid.

Because all services are delivered digitally, no physical shipping charges apply.

For delivery questions, contact: contact@prashq.com
