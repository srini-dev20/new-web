Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Prashq collects and uses information when you visit the website or contact Prashq about a project.

Prashq is a service brand operated by Srinivasan Ranganathan, based in India.

Prashq collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, discuss project scope, issue invoices, process payments, and deliver digital services.

This may include your name, email address, company name, website URL, project details, billing information, communication history, and any files or materials you choose to share for a project.

Information is used to respond to messages, prepare project scopes, deliver services, manage client communication, issue invoices, process payments, and maintain basic business records.

Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. Prashq does not store full card details or payment credentials.

Information may be shared with service providers only when needed for service delivery, payment processing, communication, compliance, or legal obligations.

Prashq does not sell personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Project materials shared by clients are used only for the purpose of evaluating, quoting, or delivering the agreed service unless separate permission is given.

Reasonable care is taken to protect information, but no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure.

To ask questions about privacy or request deletion of your information, contact: contact@prashq.com
